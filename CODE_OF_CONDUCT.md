# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Workbox is a technical project, and we intend to keep the discussion technical. Everyone who
participates, whether by opening issues, reviewing code, or asking questions, deserves to do so
without harassment, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Critique code and ideas, not people. "This path leaks a mount on the error branch" is useful.
  "Did you even test this?" is not.
- Assume competence and good faith. Most bad patches come from missing context, not carelessness.
  Supply the context.
- Be direct and be specific. Vague disapproval wastes everyone's time; a concrete objection with a
  suggested alternative moves the work forward.
- Accept review gracefully. Requested changes are part of the process, not a judgment of you.
- Respect maintainer decisions. Disagreement is fine and can be argued once, well. Relitigating
  settled decisions across multiple threads is not.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks in any project space
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Sustained disruption of discussions, including bad-faith arguing and sealioning
- Any conduct that would be clearly inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and
any official communication channel. It also applies when representing the project in public, for
example posting from a project account or speaking about the project at an event.

## Enforcement

Report violations privately to the maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md), not in
a public issue. If your report concerns a maintainer, say so in the report.
